Rules &
format

The whole league runs on a clock instead of a scoreline, so every Sunday finishes on time. Four teams, six matchups, twenty-four games.

Teams

Four teams each Sunday. Every team is two men and two women. Whoever is available that week gets entered into the four teams, so partners and opponents change every Sunday.

Matchups

Every team plays every other team once — six matchups per Sunday, two running at a time across the two courts. Every matchup is four games: one men's doubles, one women's doubles, and two mixed doubles.

The 12-minute clock

Each game is timed at exactly 12 minutes. Whichever side is ahead when the clock expires wins the game. Tied at 12 minutes goes to sudden death: the next point wins it.

Winning

Take the majority of the four games and you win the matchup. Most matchup wins on the day is the Weekly Champion. Everything resets the following Sunday with new teams.
