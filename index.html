Rules &
format

Teams

Four teams take the court each Sunday — two men and two women per side. The draw changes weekly, so fresh partnerships, new rivalries, and a little unpredictability are all part of the league.

The 12-minute clock

Every game gets twelve minutes on the clock. When time is called, the side in front takes it. If the score is level, there is no committee meeting — one sudden-death point settles the matter.

Winning

The team with the strongest Sunday record takes the weekly crown. Then the slate is wiped clean, the teams are reshuffled, and everyone gets another shot the following week.
